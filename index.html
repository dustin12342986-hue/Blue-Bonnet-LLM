INTERVAL LENS

Reads the frequency peaks in a sound and the ratios between them — which musical intervals they land nearest. Measures ratios, not meaning. The null is built in: a sound only reads as musical if its ratios cluster tighter than random. Frequencies are rounded to nearest pitch (± cents).

UNDERSTANDING

Runs every geometry on the music at once — the intervals, the difference tones, the beats, the melody — and finds, by subtraction, the real relationships it carries. A relationship is kept only if it sits at the center: a true consonance between the poles of unison and noise. Each real relationship is then crossed, on its own, into the corpus and the dreams. Nothing is summed. The meaning stays with you.
